The pressure

The commercial questions that stall a team

These are the gaps PharmSight is hired to close. They are commercial, not a copy of a manufacturing quality suite.

  • Field data, no decision

    Sales and marketing numbers land in separate files. The story of what to do next is still assembled by hand.

  • Access without evidence

    A value story is asked for before the budget impact, outcomes, and dossier are in one place.

  • A quiet competitive picture

    Pipeline moves, analyst notes, and conference signals arrive late, or not as one view.

  • Forecasts that do not travel

    In-line, pipeline, valuation, and supply models disagree because they were built apart.
